The short period, in seconds, for which the repeater remains active after the last received transmission ends. This allows the courtesy sound and a following transmission before the repeater closes the current operating session. Default 10 seconds.
The permitted duration, in seconds, of one continuous received transmission, or “over”. The timer starts when the squelch opens. If the limit is reached, a warning pip is mixed with the outgoing audio without cutting off the transmission.

When the received signal subsequently ends, SvxLink announces “Timeout”, plays the configured courtesy sound and resets the SQL timeout counter. Default 180 seconds.
