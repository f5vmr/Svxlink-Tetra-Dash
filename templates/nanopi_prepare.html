NanoPi Neo Preparation

Prepare the Armbian device-tree overlays required by the NanoPi Neo.

The NanoPi Neo requires the Armbian i2c0 and analog-codec overlays. These expose the header I²C bus and activate the H3 analogue audio codec. Any existing overlays are preserved.

A reboot is required after the boot configuration changes. Setup will return to this page automatically after the device restarts.
