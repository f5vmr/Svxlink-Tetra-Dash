Review how each enabled radio port participates in the installation.

Port linking is the final configuration-parameter stage. The enabled ports, their logic roles and the selected reflector route have already been established before connections are created.

Every enabled port must have exactly one assignment: the reflector link, one local link, or independent operation. A port cannot participate in two links at the same time.
